🏛️ RS3 Master Row Guide

⚔️ Tab 2: Combat & Armor Layout
Row 1 (Weapons)
Noxious Weapons, Sirenic/Masterwork gear pieces, Shields, Defenders, T90/T95 items.
Keep your highest-tier active weapons in slots 1 through 5.
Row 2 (Armor Sets)
Custom-Fit Trimmed Masterwork sets, Elite Sirenic gear, Capes of Accomplishment.
Line up Helm, Body, and Legs vertically under your weapons.
🧪 Tab 3: Herblore & Food Layout
Row 1 (Overloads)
Elder Overload Flasks (6), Overload (4), Aggroverload Flasks, Weapon Poison+++.
Put your 6-dose variants on the left, and lower doses right next to them.
Row 2 (Restores & Food)
Saradomin Brew Flasks (6), Super Restore Flasks (6), Sailfish, Blue Blubber Jellyfish.
Pair a row of Brews directly above or below your primary food sources.
🏃 Tab 4: Daily Runs & Runes
Row 1 (Runes & Pouches)
Rune Pouches, Air, Fire, Water, Earth, Blood, Soul, Death, and Astral runes.
Keep standard elemental runes grouped near your primary teleport jewelry.
Row 2 (Teleports & Dailies)
Passage of the Abyss, Wicked Hood, Vis Wax, Teleport Tablets.
Keep your daily teleport triggers unified in one tight group.
🗑️ Tab 9: Junk & Toy Box Cleanup
Row 1 (Holiday Clutter)
Old Easter drops, Christmas cosmetics, Hati cosmetic claws.
Right-click and select "Destroy". You can fetch them back from Diango for free.
Row 2 (Quest Leftovers)
Strange keys, quest scrolls, old ancient readable notes.
If the quest is finished, delete these or deposit them to May's Quest Caravan.
